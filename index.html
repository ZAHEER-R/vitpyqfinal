FAQ

How does the subscription plan work?

Every logged-in user gets a 1-year free subscription.
Upload 30 papers to earn a +6 months extension.
Each additional 30 uploads extends your plan further.
Your plan and end date appear on your profile.

Rewards and bonuses

New users receive 30,000 ZX.
Earn 1,000 ZX per paper upload.
Admin can grant bonus or cash prizes — credits show in your profile and legendary highlights.

Public / Private profiles

Toggle Public or Private in your profile settings.
Private accounts show only a lock message and Send Friend Request to others.
Friends and Admin can see more.

How does Chat work?

ZX Global Chat is open to all logged-in users.
Message friends privately from profile or friends list.
Be respectful and helpful.

Legendary Highlights

Admin congratulations can appear as public Legendary Highlights.
Admin can also remove a highlight later.

How do I upload a paper?

Go to Upload, fill subject, course code, slot, year, semester, campus and exam type. Preview the file, then submit.
You earn 1,000zx per paper.

How do I download papers?

Log in to view or download papers and notes. Access is free.
Your download history is private — only you and Admin can see it.

What is ZX?

ZX is the platform currency. New users get 30,000zx.
Earn 1,000zx per upload. Spend ZX in the Store on badges, designs and effects.

What can I buy in the Store?

Badges, profile design items, effects and verification.
After purchase use Apply / Remove.
Items stay in your Vault.

Friends & requests

Open Friends on your profile, tap Add Friends, search users, send requests, accept from the heart icon.

What can Admin do?

Search users, congratulate, give bonus/cash prize, terminate accounts (with reason), remove papers (with message), and manage Legendary Highlights.

Account termination

If Admin terminates your account, you will see their message and cannot log in again.
The status persists after refresh.

How do badges work?

Default is Bronze.
Buy higher badges in the Store.
The badge pin shows on your avatar across the site.

What is the Vault?

Vault stores items you purchased.
Open it from profile to apply or remove designs and custom titles.

Download privacy

Only the account owner and Admin can see download history.
Public profiles never expose downloads.

Why was this platform built?

This independent open-source community shares media, university updates and question papers with students across five campuses.

How do I upload study notes?

Go to Upload and switch to the Notes tab.
Add the subject, year, semester and campus, choose a PDF or image, preview it, then submit.
Course code is optional for notes.

How do I find a paper quickly?

Use the Exam, Semester and Year filters under Media, and pick your campus.
Bookmark papers you use often so you can reopen them from your profile.

What are Bookmarks and Liked?

Bookmarks save a paper or note for later.
Liked shows what you've liked.
Both are on your profile.

How do I rate a paper?

Open any paper and tap the stars under "Rate this paper".
Ratings help others see which papers are useful.

What if a paper is wrong, blurry or in the wrong category?

Tell us in Student Feedback or Global Chat and an admin will fix or remove it.
Papers are user-provided, so always check them before relying on them.

Can I request a paper that isn't uploaded?

Yes.
Ask in Global Chat.
If someone has it, they can upload it and earn ZX.

How does the GPA predictor work?

Open Student Tools, then GPA & CGPA predictor.
Add each subject with its credits and your expected grade, then tap Calculate GPA.
Grades at VIT are relative, so treat the result as an estimate.

How do I calculate CGPA?

Switch to the CGPA tab, add each semester's GPA and earned credits, and tap Calculate CGPA.

Can I save or share my GPA results?

Use Export / Print to save a copy or print it.

How does the timetable planner work?

Choose Theory or Lab, pick a slot such as A1, B1 or C1, and add the course.
It fills your weekly grid automatically using VIT slot timings.

What happens if two courses clash?

The planner rejects clashing slot combinations, so you'll see an error and can pick a different slot.

Can I enter combined slots like A1+TA1?

Yes, type a valid combination in the Combination field.
Invalid codes are rejected.

How do I name or export my timetable?

Give it a name at the top, then use Export / Print to get an Excel file or a PDF.

Is the timetable planner official?

No.
It's an independent student tool, so always confirm your real slots on VTOP.

Who can log in with Google?

VIT students use Google sign-in with their campus email.
Choose your campus first, since the domain depends on it.

Can I use a personal email?

Yes, register with a personal email and password.
Campus emails must continue with Google.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Tap Forgot password and enter your registered personal email to get a reset link.
VIT student accounts that use Google sign-in have no separate password to reset.

How often can I change my username?

Once every 15 days, from Edit Profile.

How do I change my name or photo?

Open your profile, tap Edit Profile, update your display name or photo, and save.

What do Public and Private profiles show?

Public profiles show your uploads, ZX, badge and stats.
Private profiles show only a lock and a Send Friend Request button.
Your download history is never public.

How do I turn on notifications?

Go to your profile and switch on Device push notifications.
Allow the browser prompt.
You'll get alerts even when the site is closed, and you can turn it off anytime.

What are announcements?

Admins post announcements for everyone or for a single campus.
They show up in notifications.

How do I send feedback?

Use Send Feedback under Student Feedback on the home page.
Admins read all of it.

Is my data safe?

The app uses only essential browser storage for sessions and preferences, with no advertising or analytics cookies.

How do I report someone in chat?

Message an admin or use Student Feedback.
