FAQ

How does the subscription plan work?

Every logged-in user gets a 1-year free subscription.
Upload 30 papers to earn a +6 months extension.
Each additional 30 uploads extends your plan further.
Your plan and end date appear on your profile.

Rewards and bonuses

New users receive 30,000 ZX.
Earn 1,000 ZX per paper upload.
Admin can grant bonus or cash prizes — credits show in your profile and legendary highlights.

Public / Private profiles

Toggle Public or Private in your profile settings.
Private accounts show only a lock message and Send Friend Request to others.
Friends and Admin can see more.

How does Chat work?

ZX Global Chat is open to all logged-in users.
Message friends privately from profile or friends list.
Be respectful and helpful.

Legendary Highlights

Admin congratulations can appear as public Legendary Highlights.
Admin can also remove a highlight later.

How do I upload a paper?

Go to Upload, fill subject, course code, slot, year, semester, campus and exam type. Preview the file, then submit.
You earn 1,000zx per paper.

How do I download papers?

Open any paper card, click Download.
Downloads are free.
Your download history is private — only you and Admin can see it.

What is ZX?

ZX is the platform currency. New users get 30,000zx.
Earn 1,000zx per upload. Spend ZX in the Store on badges, designs and effects.

What can I buy in the Store?

Badges, profile design items, effects and verification.
After purchase use Apply / Remove.
Items stay in your Vault.

Friends & requests

Open Friends on your profile, tap Add Friends, search users, send requests, accept from the heart icon.

What can Admin do?

Search users, congratulate, give bonus/cash prize, terminate accounts (with reason), remove papers (with message), and manage Legendary Highlights.

Account termination

If Admin terminates your account, you will see their message and cannot log in again.
The status persists after refresh.

How do badges work?

Default is Bronze.
Buy higher badges in the Store.
The badge pin shows on your avatar across the site.

What is the Vault?

Vault stores items you purchased.
Open it from profile to apply or remove designs and custom titles.

Download privacy

Only the account owner and Admin can see download history.
Public profiles never expose downloads.

Why was this platform built?

VIT PYQ's is for VITians by VITians — share previous year questions across Vellore, Chennai, Amaravathi, Bhopal and Bangalore, help juniors, and build community.
