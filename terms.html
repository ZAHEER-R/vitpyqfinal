VIT PYQ's Community Terms

This is an independent, open-source student community. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by VIT or any VIT campus.

Community use

  • Use the service lawfully, respectfully, and for educational community purposes.
  • Share only material you created or are permitted to distribute. Do not upload confidential, private, or personally identifying information.
  • Do not harass, impersonate, threaten, or attempt to disrupt or gain unauthorized access to accounts or services.
  • Content is shared by users. Check its accuracy and suitability before relying on it for coursework or examinations.

Content and moderation

You remain responsible for content you submit. The community may hide or remove content, or restrict accounts, when needed to protect users, rights holders, or the service.

Availability and privacy

The service is provided as available and may change. Do not use it to store sensitive information. Account and campus details are used to operate the student community.

Contact and external references

For issues, use the in-app feedback or report controls. VIT's official website is an external reference and does not imply affiliation.
