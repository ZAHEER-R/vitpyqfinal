## What is this?

VIT PYQ's is a community platform where students **share, find, and download** previous year question papers. Upload papers, earn **ZX**, upgrade your profile, chat with others, and help juniors prepare for exams.

---

## Features

### Papers
- Browse **CAT 1, CAT 2, and FAT** papers for all campuses  
- **Live search** by subject or course code  
- **Filters** — exam type, semester, year, campus  
- **New Uploads** feed on the homepage  
- **View** and **Download** free  
- **Like** papers and **Report** issues  

### Earn ZX
- **30,000 ZX** welcome bonus for new users  
- **1,000 ZX** for every paper you upload  
- **Cash prizes** and rewards from admin  
- Spend ZX in the **Store**  

### ZX Store & Vault
- Buy **badges** (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Ruby)  
- **Design items** — frames, glow, neon ring, star pad, dark pack  
- **Custom title** and **Verified** tick  
- Owned items live in your **Vault** — Apply or Remove anytime  

### Community
- **ZX Global Chat** — request papers, help others  
- **Support chat** — message the team  
- **Friends** — send requests, personal messages  
- **Notifications** — chats, rewards, friend requests in one place  
- **Student feedback** — live comments on the homepage  

### Profile
- Public or **private** profile  
- Upload and download **history**  
- Edit display name and photo  
- **Subscription plan** on your profile  

### Subscription
- **1 year free** when you log in  
- **+6 months** for every **30 papers** you upload  

### Leaderboard
- See top contributors by uploads and activity  

### Admin tools
- Manage papers and users  
- Congratulate, send cash prizes, donate ZX  
- Review and delete student feedback  
- Handle reports and account actions  

---

## How to use

1. Open the site and **register** (Gmail / Yahoo / Outlook)  
   or sign in with **Google** if you have a `@vitstudent.ac.in` email  
2. Search or filter papers on **Home**  
3. **View** → **Download** any paper  
4. **Upload** your own papers to earn ZX  
5. Visit the **Store** to upgrade badges and designs  
6. Use **Chat** and **Friends** to connect with other VITians
