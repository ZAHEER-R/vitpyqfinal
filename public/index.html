FAQ

How do I upload a paper?

Go to Upload, fill subject, course code, year, semester, campus and exam type. Preview the PDF, then submit. You earn 500 VX per paper.

What is VX?

VX is the platform currency. Earn 500 VX for every paper you share. Spend VX in the Store on badges and profile design items.

How do badges work?

Default is Bronze. Buy Silver, Gold, Platinum or Diamond from the Store. The badge appears as a pin on your profile picture everywhere.
